# Concept Note - Blue Tokai Easy Pour - specialty coffee, just a mug and hot water

**Project:** blue-tokai-easy-pour-20s  
**Concept revision:** 1  
**Brief revision:** 1  
**Content family:** ad  
**Duration / frame:** 20.0s, 25 fps, 16:9

## Brief

> Blue Tokai Easy Pour - 20-second YouTube ad.  Objective: drive first-time online purchases of Blue Tokai Easy Pour. Feature the Mixed Bag so people new to specialty coffee can try roasts ranging from light to dark. bluetokaicoffee.com  Target audience: coffee drinkers in India who want specialty coffee at home or at work without buying brewing equipment. Busy professionals who already buy cafe coffee or drink instant coffee at home and are curious about easy pour-over.  Core message: Specialty coffee. Just a mug and hot water. Show the Easy Pour filter clipped to a mug, the water hitting the grounds, and the finished cup. The filter is built into the sachet, so the demo makes the benefit visible.  Call to action: Shop Easy Pour. Pair the CTA with the Mixed Bag pack and the Blue Tokai logo on the end card.

## Audience

Busy Indian coffee drinkers who want specialty coffee at home or work without buying equipment. They already buy cafe coffee or instant coffee and are curious about easy pour-over. This is the brief's audience, not a measured study.

## Objective

Make the built-in-filter benefit visible and drive a first Easy Pour purchase, with the Mixed Bag as the entry product.

## Evidence

Blue Tokai's product pages establish the Mixed Bag assortment and the named Vienna and Attikan packs, with Seethargundu added from the Mixed Bag page to complete the light end of the range. The Rich & Bold Trio link is recorded as a dark-range reference only; it is not Easy Pour. The brewing page establishes the real tear-anchor-pour method and the two-minute steep. Google's creative guidance supports early attention, early branding and a clear CTA. Price, pack size and public image rights remain unapproved.

**Recommendation:** CO-01.

### CO-01 - No machine. Just a mug.

**Hook:** Specialty coffee. Just a mug and hot water.

Open tight on a Blue Tokai Mixed Bag pack on a kitchen counter, with a mug already in frame. The hook lands as the sachet is lifted. Cut to hands tearing the outer sachet and the built-in filter, anchoring it over the mug. Water hits the grounds in macro; the bed blooms. A labelled time-lapse carries the repeated pours and steep. The film opens out to the Mixed Bag, Seethargundu, Attikan and Vienna packs, then lands on the finished cup and the end card: logo, Mixed Bag and Shop Easy Pour.

**Visual style:** Warm daylight kitchen, honest hands, macro water and coffee texture, clean cream end card. Blue Tokai blue and pack colours carry the frame. No lab equipment, no espresso machine, no fake UI.

**Sound:** Dry kitchen ambience, a soft pack crinkle, a clear water pour, a low music bed that lifts at the range reveal, and a quiet brand sting. Sparse VO: Specialty coffee. Tear, anchor, pour. Just a mug and hot water. Light to dark. One mug. One cup. Shop Easy Pour.

**Why:** The product benefit is physical, so the strongest ad is the action itself. One continuous demo proves the built-in filter without a category lecture, and the range reveal gives the first-time buyer a reason to choose the Mixed Bag.

**Risks:** Hands and brew footage need a performer release and a controlled food set.; Public pack images are not yet cleared for paid media.; The repeated pours and two-minute steep must be compressed and disclosed.

### CO-02 - Light to dark

**Hook:** One bag. Light to dark.

A colour-graded triptych: Seethargundu in cool morning light, Attikan in warm afternoon, Vienna in low evening light. Each chapter shows a finished cup and a pack, then resolves into the Mixed Bag. The CTA closes on the range.

**Visual style:** Editorial coffee-table photography, strong colour chapters, slow lateral moves, minimal type.

**Sound:** Three musical textures that resolve into one bed; no VO until the close.

**Why:** It makes the range tangible and premium, but it spends the 20 seconds on roast character rather than the built-in-filter benefit the brief asks to demonstrate.

**Risks:** Needs more product and cup setups.; The demo benefit becomes secondary.; Harder to prove 'no equipment' visually.

### CO-03 - The 3pm desk brew

**Hook:** 3pm. Still no machine.

A character at a desk hits the afternoon slump, opens the Mixed Bag, anchors the filter to a mug and pours. The finished cup lands next to the laptop; the end card closes on the pack and CTA.

**Visual style:** Bright observational comedy, office detail, handheld energy, bold captions.

**Sound:** Playful percussion, comic reaction, a clean brand sting.

**Why:** It makes the audience concrete, but adds casting, performance and a set. It also risks making the coffee look like an office shortcut rather than a specialty product.

**Risks:** Casting and performance add cost and revision risk.; The product benefit competes with the joke.; The premium specialty cue can get lost.

## Hook options

| ID | Hook | Why | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| HK-01 | **Specialty coffee. Just a mug and hot water.** | States the category and the benefit in one line, matches the brief's core message, and sets up the demo. | 'Just' can sound too casual for a premium product. |
| HK-02 | **No machine. No barista. Just coffee.** | Names the barriers the audience cares about and creates a clean before/after. | 'No barista' may imply cafe coffee is the enemy. |
| HK-03 | **One sachet. One cup. Light to dark.** | Combines convenience and range, and gives the edit a natural three-part structure. | Leads with format rather than the specialty-coffee promise. |

## Why this treatment

**Hook:** HK-01 says the category and the benefit in one line. It works as spoken and on-screen copy and does not depend on price, promotion or an unverified claim.

**Script:** The script follows the physical demo: name the category, show the three actions, state the benefit, reveal the range, ask for the purchase. It avoids calling the product instant coffee.

**Look:** A warm kitchen and macro water make the product action legible. The pack labels stay unobstructed, and the range shot uses the actual Mixed Bag, Seethargundu, Attikan and Vienna packs rather than invented variants.

**Sound:** The pour and pack crinkle carry the demo; the music lifts only at the range reveal and the brand sting closes the film. Captions carry the message with sound off.

## Planned look

Warm daylight kitchen, deep blue and cream brand accents, macro water and coffee texture, clean pack photography, a cream end card with the Blue Tokai logo, Mixed Bag pack and CTA.

## Success definition

A viewer can repeat the product benefit after one sound-off view: specialty coffee, a built-in filter, just a mug and hot water. The end card names Easy Pour and the Mixed Bag. Performance targets are not invented at Level 1.

## Viewing context

YouTube 16:9, likely watched on a phone in a feed or in-stream; assume sound off for the first view and a skippable opening. A 9:16 cut is a separate placement decision.

## Reference notes

No competitor video was used as a creative template. The treatment borrows the public principle of a tactile product demonstration and YouTube's ABCD guidance; it does not reuse any third-party footage or audio.

## What would reverse this route

Reverse the demo-led route if a controlled test shows the brew action reads as too slow, if the built-in filter cannot be shown clearly at phone scale, or if brand approval requires a pack-only end card.

## Prohibited content

No 'instant coffee' label, no invented equipment, no price or promotion, no unapproved health or superiority claim, no third-party footage, no fake UI.

## Canonical script

Specialty coffee. Tear, anchor, pour. Just a mug and hot water. Light to dark. One mug. One cup. Shop Easy Pour.

## Open questions

- Hero pack size: brand decision; blocks pack layout, price and end-card copy.
- CTA destination: brand decision; blocks tracking and publishing, not the wording.
- Cleared pack/logo masters: brand rights owner; blocks all pack and logo renders.
- 9:16 Shorts variant: media owner; blocks a separate vertical deliverable.
- Human or synthetic narration: brand producer; blocks voice casting and disclosure.
- Brand typeface: brand design owner; blocks final typography and HI-05 clearance.
