Blue Tokai Easy Pour — 20-second YouTube ad brief

Objective
Drive first-time online purchases of Blue Tokai Easy Pour. Feature the Mixed
Bag so people new to specialty coffee can try roasts ranging from light to dark.
bluetokaicoffee.com

Target audience
Coffee drinkers in India who want to make specialty coffee at home or at work
without buying brewing equipment. Focus on busy professionals who already buy
café coffee or drink instant coffee at home and are curious about an easy
pour-over.

Core message
“Specialty coffee. Just a mug and hot water.” Show the Easy Pour filter clipped
to a mug, the water hitting the grounds, and the finished cup. The filter is
built into the sachet, so the demo makes the benefit visible.

Call to action
Shop Easy Pour. Pair the CTA with the Mixed Bag pack and the Blue Tokai logo on
the end card.

Supplied references
Blue Tokai Coffee Roasters logo
Easy Pour Mixed Bag — featured product
Easy Pour Vienna — dark roast
Easy Pour Attikan Estate — medium-dark roast
